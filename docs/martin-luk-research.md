# Martin Luk's trading method: research notes behind Kabu's Martin Luk track

These are the working notes used to write the Martin Luk lessons in Kabu. They summarise what Martin Luk says about his own process, with a source and a time for every point, so each lesson can be checked against the original video.

## Method

- Sources are Martin's own words: his YouTube channel ([@martinlukkt](https://www.youtube.com/@martinlukkt)) and three long interviews.
- Captions were pulled for all 115 videos on the channel (live streams, Nov 2025 to Nov 2026). 100 had captions; 15 had none on YouTube and could not be used.
- The two TraderLion interviews were read in full. Streams were read in full where he explains his routine (premarket, watchlists, weekend scans, watchlist rules) and searched by topic for the rest (risk per trade, sizing, stops, break-even, the open, earnings, liquidity, scans, shorts, selling).
- Where his statements changed over time, both versions are kept with dates.
- Two videos by James Muninn, who logged Martin's 2026 trades, were used only to cross-check and are marked as secondary.
- Captions are automatic: times are accurate to about a minute, and tickers or numbers are sometimes misheard.

## Background
- Started Oct 2020 with ~US$1,300 (HK$10k); tripled in 4 months buying hyped names (GME at the top, Jan 2021). [969 0:06]
- 2021: "death by a thousand cuts", ~50% drawdown, 5-6 red months. Found Kristjan Kullamägi late 2021 (mentor via his streams, no paid service). Read Minervini first (VCP, risk mgmt, stops). [969 0:07-0:09]
- 2022 bear: barely traded, ~flat. June 2023 CFLT ("Confluent"?) breakout +100% in 2 weeks put equity at a new high after 2.5 yr drawdown = turning point. [969 0:10; 283 0:18]
- Studied economics & finance at uni (Hong Kong); says it didn't help, maybe hurt. [969 0:05]
- 2024 USIC: entered Feb, 283% official (340% incl Jan). 30% drawdown May-Sep 2024 during bank internship, rule breaking, random trades. [283 0:23]
- 2025 USIC: 969%. 209 winners / 731 losers (~22% win rate). 26% drawdown Dec 2025 (overtrading ~70-80 trades, flipping long/short). [969 0:20-0:51]
- Trades from Hong Kong; US open 9:30pm/10:30pm HK, close 4-5am → can't watch closes; prefers names that reverse in first 1-2 hrs. [969 1:27, 2:53; 283 1:12]

## Style summary
- Swing trader, trend follower. Setups: breakouts, EPs, parabolic long/short (from Kullamägi) + pullbacks (added 2024-25, learned from the short side first). [969 0:13-0:15; 283 0:25]
- Tight stop + low win rate. Win rate 20-25%; doesn't watch it closely. [969 0:24]
- "Can I manage risk here? If yes, it's a good setup" (quoting Nick/@...). Can't predict return, can limit risk. [283 0:27-0:28]
- Holding: winners avg ~5 days (2024), 1-3 weeks if working; shorts 2-3 days. [283 0:24-0:27; 969 0:25]
- Indicators: EMAs 9/21/50 (+150 added), dollar volume, anchored VWAP. Same EMAs on all timeframes. [969 0:26, 0:30]

## Risk & sizing
- Risk ~0.5% of account per trade (average); less when trades not working, more with cushion/conviction. [969 0:16; 283 0:25]
- Position size mostly 25-30%; max 35% (2024 rule); small/micro caps 20-25% because of gap risk; slow names (GLD/SLV, index) can exceed 35%. [969 0:16-0:17; 283 0:25]
- Stop ≤ 50% of the stock's ADR, hard max 5%; in practice rarely >3% or 2.5%; "1.5% is the sweet spot". [969 0:17-0:18, 0:33; 283 0:26]
- Sizing math: $10k account, 0.5% = $50 risk, stop 2.5% → $2,000 = 20% position. 1.5% stop ≈ 33%... (he says ~20-25% at 1.5% w/ 0.5%? check: 0.5/1.5=33%). [969 0:33-0:35, 3:03-3:05]  NOTE he misspeaks; use the formula.
- Sizes in % of current equity (compounding), learned from Kullamägi. [969 3:05]
- Margin: up to ~280% long at peak 2025; 3-4 positions normally, 6-7 trending, max ~11. Margin grows naturally as new trades work; no margin cap, the per-trade risk cap does the job. [969 0:41-0:44]
- Rarely adds to winners (adds tended to fail; tight stop already gives full size). [283 0:54]
- Avoids chasing: skips if price >3% above low of day / ideal entry. [969 0:24]
- 2026 change (stream, 7 Jan 2026 IgiOMLGVD_A [79]): risk 0.3-0.4% per trade instead of 0.5%. (Sep 2026 soTk48wKouE [146-147]): now 0.3% risk, 20-25% positions (was 25-30% / 0.5%) to cut drawdown in chop; slippage at 7-figure size.

## Tight-stop maths (his slide)
- For a +25% trade, cutting stop 3%→1.5% adds ~8.3R; +50% → +17R; +75% → +25R. Parabolic relationship. [969 0:31; 283 0:29]
- One 20-30R winner pays for 20-30 losses (INOD example). [969 3:08]
- Downsides: volatile equity curve, "behavioral slippage" after strings of losses, more trades. "Pick your poison"; don't use if it doesn't suit you. [969 0:38-0:40]
- Big winners rarely revisit the low of the breakout day (Amazon 90s, Adobe, FSLR 2007, PLUG 2013): basis for low-of-day stops. [283 0:33-0:35]

## Entries
- Prior-day high breakout (esp. after inside day; "every start of a move involves the breakout of prior day highs"). [283 0:36, 1:23]
- Opening range high: 1-min ORH right after open (before first 5-min candle forms); after that 5-min. Stop = low of day. [283 0:38-0:40]
- Intraday range high re-entry after failed ORH (prefers 5-min: less noise). [283 0:39]
- Pullback intraday trigger: wait for fast flush into support (hourly 9/21, daily 9/21, AVWAP, prior highs, gap) then buy first breakout of previous bar high: 1-min bars first 15 min, 5-min from 15-60 min, 15-min later. [969 1:56-1:58]
- Stops: low of day ~60-70% of time; else low of breakout bar / previous bar if LOD too wide; sometimes opening price. Max distance 5% (2024) → rule: if LOD >5% away use 5-min entry candle low. [969 1:58-1:59, 2:59; 283 0:37-0:38]
- Always sets the stop before entering. [969 3:40]
- Wick play / gap into upper wick of prior day (Oliver Kell idea). [969 3:12; 283 1:00]
- Market orders ~90% of the time. [stream xZ5LigAsWec 29 Jul 2026 [99]]
- Doesn't consider volume on entries (only for parabolics). [stream cxh5np47P9w 27 Oct 2026 [37-38]]

## Pullback setup (2025 core)
- Why: earlier entry inside base, tighter stop, avoids chasing, better average price, clean V reactions in 2025. [969 1:00-1:03]
- Where: daily 9/21 EMA, AVWAP, prior highs/lows, unfilled gaps, 50/150 EMA — best when several levels line up ("highest edge"). [969 1:09-1:10, 1:40]
- Best: first pullback within a base that has higher lows / first break-and-retest after breaking above AVWAP. [969 1:17, 2:04]
- Wants 9 above 21 & close together; nothing in "middle of nowhere"; at least daily 9 EMA (hourly-only pullbacks = his December mistakes). [969 1:24, 1:53, 0:49-0:50]
- Pullback to daily 9 has the lowest win rate; slower EMA (21) higher win rate (his own study). [969 3:19]
- Loves gap up then reversal / weak open falling into support then fast V recovery; dislikes slow late-day recoveries (HK time). [969 1:25, 1:41]
- Weekly chart to judge context (weekly 9>21, weekly pin bar + inside week). [969 1:32-1:37, 3:06]

## Anchored VWAP
- Learned from Brian Shannon (TraderLion interview). Anchors to swing high/low of base, huge gap/volume day, big reversal bar; adjusts anchor to bar price respects most. Daily/hourly for multi-day; intraday AVWAP on 1/5-min. [969 1:06-1:50]
- Uses: break above AVWAP then retest = pullback buy; basing above AVWAP = constructive; repeated support on AVWAP while daily ugly = early tell (SNDK). [969 1:30-1:39]

## Shorts
- Pullback short = flip of pullback long: rally into declining 9/21/50 EMAs + AVWAP when EMAs converging; stop at high of day. Enters near resistance, doesn't wait for prior-day low break. [283 1:42-1:43; 969 2:06]
- Ideal: first breakdown below 50 EMA, then first pullback into the 50 with 9/21 near and curling down, overshoot at best. [969 3:43-3:44]
- Takes profits faster on shorts (2-3 days, sells into weakness); maths: 50% then 50% = 75% on shorts vs 125% on longs. R:R lower than longs. [969 3:36-3:37, 3:42]
- Don't short just on daily 9/21 when weekly still above 9/50 (RKLB lesson). [969 2:08]
- Lesson 2025: short less, only high quality / market below all MAs. Study: Q's above 50 EMA? IWM leading Q's → since 2008, 0 of the >15% Q corrections happened while IWM was leading. [969 3:42, 3:57-4:00]

## Parabolics
- TQQQ long Apr 2025 after 3 gap downs: 20% below 1-hr EMA, Q's 15% below daily 9; bought 1-min ORH/intraday breakout, stop low of breakout bar ~2%, target hourly 9 EMA, sold all into strength near 5-min 150 EMA; 13R in 30 min. Planned in advance. [969 2:36-2:42]
- Parabolic long criteria (stream 29 Jul 2026 xZ5LigAsWec [87-89]): steep slope, wide-range candles, volume expansion.
- Dollar volume peaking on a parabolic = sell (LMND, QBTS 2024). [283 1:17, 1:23]

## Selling
- No strict sell rule. Sell 10-15% (2024) / ~15-20% (2025) per trim into strength when up 3R or 5R or extended on hourly; close rest on first close below 9 EMA (sometimes 21/50 early in move). [969 2:27-2:29, 2:56; 283 0:40-0:43]
- Sells into strength more: early in move, market extended for months, in drawdown; trails more when equity at highs / mid-trend. [283 0:44-0:47; 969 2:56]
- Can't watch close → sets stop a little below 9 EMA at a bar low ("half stop") to allow undercut. [969 2:54]
- Euphoria = sign of top. [283 0:42]
- Knows his weakness: sells too early (HOOD, SMCI). Prefers controlling downside over maximising upside. [969 2:46-2:48]
- Rarely moves stop to breakeven (stream Aug 2026 DZNRjMvhFUw batch [123]). Check.

## Screening / scans / watchlists
- Three scans [283 1:30-1:34]: (1) Premarket gappers on big volume (10-15 min before open); (2) "Potent" scan = biggest % gainers previous day (do first; look for clusters in same industry = theme); (3) "Leader" scan = weekends, sort by 1-month performance to catch missed RS leaders.
- Theme detection: several names from same industry in scans / several upgrades within a day. [969 2:31-2:33; 283 1:32]
- Watchlists [283 1:34-1:40]: Main (holdings, ideas for tomorrow bull/bear, key stocks, QQQ/SPY benchmark); Leading (9>21>50 strong uptrend); Mediocre (no clear trend, basing near EMAs; may keep if weekly fine/relative strength); Lagging (downtrend); Pillar (mega caps: AAPL, NVDA, MSFT…); Tier 2 (growth & micro caps); "Premier" section = avg dollar volume ≥ $100M vs illiquid.
- Count of names in leading vs lagging lists = breadth gauge. [283 1:36; 969 3:50]
- Fast movers: ADR > 5% (2024). Prefers higher dollar volume/liquidity in same theme. [283 0:26; 969 3:18]
- Prioritising in a theme: relative strength (weekly and last 1-3 days), tightness/inside bar, dollar volume. [969 3:16-3:19]
- Routine: premarket 45-60 min before open; scans + go through all watchlists + positions. 30 min in strong market, 15-20 min in correction. [969 3:52; 283 1:34]
- EPs: doesn't look at fundamentals, only headline. [283 1:13]
- Study: mostly during market hours (scrolling charts testing ideas), avoids charts outside hours now. [969 3:54-3:56]

## Market / situational awareness
- Order: (1) own trade feedback (most important), (2) indices QQQ & IWM daily, are they at resistance (declining 21/50)? skip good setups then, (3) watchlist breadth, (4) pillar mega caps (MSFT failing in Feb 2024 = warning). [969 3:47-3:51; 283 1:38-1:39, 1:55-1:58]
- Best time to be aggressive = pullbacks of the indices to rising EMAs, not after months of extension. [969 3:48-3:50]

## Psychology / lessons
- Journal every trade; losses teach more than wins. [283 1:59; 969 1:52]
- Weaknesses: revenge trading, raising risk in losing streak, ignoring stops (fixed 2 & 3; hard stops every trade, size down on streak). [283 2:01-2:02]
- It's okay to miss leaders. "What would the great traders do?" eliminates ~90% of random trades. Trading as a business/marathon (message to himself in 2050). [969 4:01-4:05; 283 2:08-2:12]
- Copy a mentor first, then adapt to your personality. [969 0:36-0:37; 283 2:00]

## Stream evidence (2025-26) — evolution and extra detail
- Watchlist rules (11 Feb 2026 h9QSoeXx7eM 1:14-1:18; 12 Mar 2026 ZBJU2uGgrg0 1:12-1:15): Leading = price > 50 EMA AND 50 EMA > 150 EMA. Lagging = price < 50 AND 50 < 150. Mediocre = in between (usually price between 50 & 150). Changed from older 9>21>50 rule; kept simple, daily chart only, ~30 s to classify. 50 EMA = watershed: above it only look long, below only short.
- Lists come 99% from screeners; adds strongest names after a big day, then waits 2-3 weeks for pullback ("handshake" entries) (h9Q 1:20-1:29). Potent scan run right after US close (HK morning) when calm (ZBJU 1:09). ~100 charts in potent scan (AntPf0JXhbw 0:49-0:50). Reviews existing watchlists before screeners (AntPf 0:04).
- Liquidity floors: watchlist ≥ $100M avg daily dollar volume, premarket scanner 50M (56ECvN_79n0 25 Nov 2025 0:19-0:23); "floor rising" (vN8yKUoA7gs 29 Apr 2026 0:17); tier-2 raised to $200M (-5l8H8TBF5s 21 May 2026 1:37); prefers ≥$500M/day or ≥$10B mkt cap (603RKPXwd3Y 20 May 2026 2:08). Tier 2 = small caps < $5B (AntPf 10 Aug 2026 0:10-0:12) — trades small caps less: shakeouts, rarely multi-week moves. Small-cap list ≥$50M, remove below (h9Q 1:20-1:21, earlier).
- Dollar volume indicator: "Total dollar volume" by Raffle Trades on TradingView, used 3-4 yrs (BTZ9bk3JVhA 23 Oct 2026 1:52). Tried Deepvue (no futures, no desktop) (BTZ9 2:58). Finviz in 2020-21 (t8DxEdoPhXE 0:35). Broker IBKR (NhlSoNsb5vg 1:05-1:06).
- Fundamentals: ~90% technical/10% theme; can't read financial reports (4Kt7AtQ4Dto 9 Apr 2026 1:59-2:03). EP: only headline (283 1:13).
- No RSI/MACD; doesn't use extension indicators; eyeballs (ufN4u_ncWZg 28 Aug 2026 1:47).
- Breadth: count of his three watchlists, not RSP/NH-NL (BTZ9 1:43).
- Risk evolution: 0.5% (2024-25) → 0.3-0.4% (Jan 2026) → ~0.3%, sometimes 0.1-0.25% (Jul-Aug 2026 BRht1eWBAIY 2:12; K5bjqj9GBUE 2:00). Says if he restarted he'd use 0.3% for this low-win-rate style (OBQEWy8PAck 25 Jun 2026 1:47). No daily max-loss limit (OBQ 1:46). Small account: maybe fewer trades, 1-2% risk (SRbfFeUeWeY 1:30).
- Sizes: 20-25% typical in 2026, rarely >30% (K5bj 2:15; soTk48wKouE 17 Sep 2026 [138-147]).
- Slow size-up: double 10k→20k, risk based on ~15k (uses ~half unrealized gains); so no need to cut size in a mild drawdown (HM79DK83psE 2 Jun 2026 1:41-1:50; Q1Deuj_Rkso 1:20-1:21). Oct 2026: still sizing off the earlier account size after a fast +30% (YPMzwyRIMhI 2:04-2:06).
- Quality-based risk: only slight changes (0.25% → 0.2% for B setup) (Q1De 1:18-1:19).
- Never raise risk in a drawdown — biggest 2024 mistake (yvaMXQfOEWI 26 May 2026 2:14).
- Breakeven: Feb 2026 says moves stop to BE once 9 EMA crosses above entry (h9Q 0:56); Jun-Aug 2026 says rarely moves stops / rarely to BE (E24lil89pj8 0:34; K5bj 2:03; ufN4 2:37). Don't like moving stops after entry, could be bad habit (Muninn quoting stream).
- First 15 minutes: since 2023 very choppy; in 2026 avoids trading first 15 min, prefers 15-min pullback, ideally undercut of opening range low then intraday breakout (h9Q 0:36-0:37; wiyv0ISHiHk 0:26; WwGjyp-XC4I 0:55; Muninn quoting Feb 2026 Cantonese interview).
- Earnings: generally won't open a trade within 5 days of earnings; holds through only with cushion ~5% above stop (big/low-reaction names), ~10% for small caps, 7-8% for volatile ones (looks at last 5 earnings gaps); else sells half or all (cu2qxjXIfPc 5 May 2026 1:18-1:21; YXWBRbXQGBk 26 Oct 2026 0:20-0:22).
- Trims: "rarely trim now" early in uptrend; 15-20% into strength whenever he wants (cu2q 1:22). Tries to sell 3 of 5 positions into strength, keep strongest 2 (cxh5np47P9w 27 Oct 2026 [66-68]). Selling into strength keeps equity curve & mental capital at highs.
- Exits only in regular hours; market orders ~90% (xZ5LigAsWec 29 Jul 2026 [99],[143-144]).
- Shorts: prefers inverse/levered ETFs (compounding) e.g. SOXS; UVXY for quick selloffs (BTZ9 0:46-0:55). Don't short laggard in strong sector; want weak sector (Muninn quoting). Shorting momentum stocks needs good timing; beginners should only short the lagging list (h9Q 1:23-1:33).
- Breakout day factors: general market (most important), sector, distribution/accumulation, RS & higher lows, weekly chart (ZBJU 1:20-1:24).
- Setups secondary: big picture — momentum, higher lows, fast enough, liquid enough, RS vs indices (KBcth12Ofgc clip).
- Market regime: IWM leading Qs = breadth healthy (Q1De 1:39-1:41); since 2008 every big Q correction began with IWM relative weakness — "necessary not sufficient" (Muninn quoting).
- Drawdown recovery: after Dec 2025 26% DD stopped 2-3 days, restarted small (h9Q 0:11-0:13).
- Journal: wrote daily journal first ~2.5 yrs; now just Excel log + occasional notes (ZBJU 0:59-1:00; BTZ9 2:46-2:50). Works on weaknesses over strengths (BTZ9 2:29).
- Advice: don't learn trading while working 9-5 with family—probability low; he had uni free time (0bvjF-T_3UE/XKO… 3 Mar 2026 [69-72]). Avoid social media opinions (HbBRhvwW6tM 1:20; xZ5L [145-149]). Use 0.3% risk when new (fGPCqWLQ-Qk 1:13).
- Parabolic long checklist (xZ5L [87-89]): steep slope, wide ranges, volume expansion. Parabolic top shorts: huge weekly volume, first reversal then retest of 9 EMA (gold/silver Jan 2026) (ZBJU 1:31-1:39).
- Tight stop + 0.3% risk + 20-25% size on 7-figure account; slippage handled by smaller size, considering splitting entries (ufN4 2:34-2:35; Q1De 1:16-1:17).
